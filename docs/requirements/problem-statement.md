# Study Abroad Hub

## Problem Statement

Students planning to pursue higher education abroad often need
to collect information from multiple university websites.
Finding and comparing program details, tuition fees, academic
requirements, and eligibility criteria can be time-consuming.

Study Abroad Hub is proposed as a centralized web-based system
that allows students to explore selected study-abroad programs,
search and filter them according to their preferences, and
check their basic eligibility using predefined rules.

## Objectives

1. To provide information about selected universities and programs.
2. To enable students to search and filter study programs.
3. To check student eligibility based on academic requirements.
4. To display important program information in an organized manner.
5. To allow students to save programs of interest.

## Scope

The initial system will work with a controlled dataset of
selected universities and programs.

It will focus on program discovery, filtering, eligibility
checking, and shortlisting.

An AI-powered assistant using Retrieval-Augmented Generation
(RAG) may be added as an optional enhancement.

## Intended Users

- Students exploring study-abroad opportunities.
- Administrator responsible for maintaining program information.
